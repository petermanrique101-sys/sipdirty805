What is a Dirty Soda?

It's your favorite soda… but better.

A dirty soda is a fun, customizable drink made by mixing soda or energy drinks with flavored syrups, fresh fruit, and creamy add-ins like half & half or cold foam. At Sip Dirty 805 we take it to the next level.
